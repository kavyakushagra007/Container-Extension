Privacy Policy for SaveIt

Last updated: November 2026

SaveIt ("we", "our", or "extension") is designed with complete user privacy in mind. We believe your notes, clipped text, and bookmarks belong exclusively to you.

1. Data Collection & Storage

SaveIt is local-first. We do NOT sell, track, or share your personal data with third-party advertisers.

  • Local Storage: All saved text snippets, links, tags, and Web Memory browsing records are stored locally on your computer using Chrome's chrome.storage.local API.
  • Optional Cloud Sync: If you explicitly choose to create an account and enable Cloud Sync, your saved items and categories are securely synchronized via Google Firebase Firestore REST APIs. This is completely optional and disabled by default.

2. Permissions Justification

  • storage: Required to save your clips, notes, and preferences locally on your machine.
  • contextMenus: Enables the right-click "Save to SaveIt" option on selections, links, and pages.
  • activeTab & scripting: Allows the extension to extract the selected text snippet when you invoke the keyboard shortcut (⌘+Shift+S / Ctrl+Shift+S) or context menu.
  • clipboardRead: Used only when you voluntarily click "Paste from clipboard" to save copied text.
  • tabs: Used to detect active tab title and URL for saved clips and chronological Web Memory recall.
  • history: Used exclusively for the optional "Web Memory" feature to allow users to search recent browsing visits and promote visited pages to permanent saves. Your history is processed locally on your machine and is never transmitted to any third party.
  • Host Permissions (Google Firebase): Connects directly to Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore only when you sign in for optional multi-device cloud backup.

3. Third-Party Sharing

We do not partner with third-party advertisers, analytics services, or trackers. There are zero tracking pixels, telemetry scripts, or analytics tools included in SaveIt.

4. Data Control & Deletion

You have 100% control over your data at all times:

  • You can export all your data at any time in standard JSON format.
  • You can delete individual items or use "Clear All" in the Dashboard to wipe all data permanently.
  • Uninstalling the extension instantly removes all stored local data.

5. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can open an issue on the project's repository or contact the developer.
